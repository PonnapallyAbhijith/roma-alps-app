International Driving Permit

Needed forDriving the Bari → Polignano → Alberobello → Matera → Ostuni → Lecce → Bari route
WhoWhoever will be driving (Abhijith)
Where to get itAny RTO, or via India Post/Parivahan Sewa online
Lead timeUsually a few working days — apply well before 11 Oct
Italy expects non-EU visitors to carry an International Driving Permit alongside their Indian licence when driving or renting a car — an Indian licence alone often isn't accepted by rental counters or by police at a routine stop. It's cheap and quick to get but needs your passport, licence, and photos, so it's worth doing early rather than the week before.
